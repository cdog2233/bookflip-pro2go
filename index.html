1. Identify the book

Enter an ISBN-10 or ISBN-13, or use the iPhone camera scanner. Book details are checked against free catalogs. If an ISBN has no match, enter the title and author manually and search by those words.

Search Amazon Books
Lookup tries Google Books and then Open Library. Amazon search opens book-category results using the ISBN, or the title and author after you select a match. Always confirm the exact edition, binding and ISBN yourself.

2. Confirm listing & condition

Use the matching Amazon product page to verify the exact edition and seller eligibility.

3. Calculate the deal

Inventory workflow

Each book has a separate workflow stage (Research, To buy, Purchased, Listed, Sold or Skipped). The BUY / REVIEW / PASS result is only the deal calculator recommendation and does not block you from recording a purchase.

Use Mark purchased or change the workflow dropdown in Inventory. Purchased cost initially copies your estimated purchase cost; update the cost in a later version if the amount you actually paid differs.

Backup your records

Records are stored in this browser on this device. They do not sync to your other devices yet. Export CSV regularly.
