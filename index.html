How it calculates
Net = sale price − Amazon referral / closing / fulfillment / other fees − retail cost − inbound shipping − tax / other acquisition costs. ROI = net ÷ landed acquisition cost. Maximum buy assumes your minimum profit target from Settings.

Free · on-device photo reading

Book Photo Scanner

Take up to four photos of a book. BookFlip reads visible text on your device, looks for a valid ISBN, and can search free book catalogs. No paid AI service or API key is required.

Your photos are processed in this browser and are not uploaded to BookFlip or saved in your inventory. The first scan may download the free OCR language model. For best results, photograph the front cover and the ISBN/barcode or copyright page in bright, even light.
Up to 4 photos. JPEG, PNG or WebP. Keep the ISBN close and in focus.

Import historical snapshots

Import a normalized CSV with headers date, used_price, sales_rank, used_offers, source. This creates dated observations without changing your book records.

This CSV option uses BookFlip's normalized template; it does not parse Keepa Product Finder exports as historical series.

Import Keepa API product JSON

Import a JSON response saved from Keepa's Product API with history enabled. This reads the product's csv history arrays for USED price, SALES rank, and COUNT_USED offer count. Amazon.com (domain ID 1) is supported.

This imports a file you provide; it does not call Keepa or retrieve live data. Keepa Time is converted to dates. Unavailable negative prices/ranks are skipped, while zero used-offer counts are retained. Official Keepa Product Object format.

Inventory workflow

Each book has a separate workflow stage (Research, To buy, Purchased, Listed, Sold or Skipped). The BUY / REVIEW / PASS result is only the deal calculator recommendation and does not block you from recording a purchase.

Use Mark purchased or change the workflow dropdown in Inventory. When you move a book to Purchased, Listed or Sold, BookFlip asks what you actually paid so realized profit does not silently rely on an estimate. Use Mark purchased again if you need to correct that amount.

Backup your records

Records are stored in this browser on this device. They do not sync to your other devices yet. Export CSV regularly.
