2. Compare retailer purchase prices

Open a retailer search for this exact ISBN, verify the product/edition and seller, then enter the current price. The app does not pretend to scrape prices that retailers do not expose.

Retailer prices, stock, coupons, shipping and taxes must be checked on the retailer page. Enter the price you can actually purchase at; do not rely on search snippets alone.

3. Verify the Amazon listing & demand

Match the exact edition before trusting the numbers. Enter current observations from the Amazon listing, Seller app, or Keepa. These are manual snapshots—not live data pulled by BookSource Pro.

A sales-rank number alone does not guarantee sales. Prefer recent rank history, days with sales, current competing offers, your condition, and your own eligibility check. Never assume a book is eligible because the listing exists.
